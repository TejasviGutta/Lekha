# Lekha 

**AI-powered autocorrect for Telugu written using English letters.**

Lekha is a project focused on making Telugu communication easier for people who can speak the language but struggle to read or write it. Using AI and machine learning, we aim to develop an autocorrect system that learns individual users' writing patterns and helps them type Telugu words using the English alphabet.

Our goal is to bridge the communication gap between young adults in the United States and their families back home by making it easier to text in Telugu without needing to type in Telugu script.

## The Problem

Autocorrect is an essential part of everyday communication in English. However, when people use English letters to type words from their native language, traditional English autocorrect often changes those words into unintended English words or incorrect spellings.

Many young adults in the United States speak Telugu at home but are more comfortable reading and writing in English. Although they want to communicate with family members in Telugu, typing Telugu words can be difficult when existing keyboards and autocorrect systems do not recognize their intended words.

**Lekha aims to make typing Telugu in English letters as effortless as texting in English.**

## Our Vision

We envision an AI-powered autocorrect system that understands Telugu words written using the English alphabet, learns how individual users spell those words, and helps them communicate naturally.

Our initial target use case is messaging on WhatsApp, particularly conversations between young adults in the United States and their families back home.

## Scope and Language Format

Lekha focuses exclusively on Telugu for its initial development.

Instead of requiring users to type in Telugu script (తెలుగు), Lekha focuses on **Telugu transliteration**: writing Telugu words phonetically using English letters.

Examples:

| Telugu Written in English Letters | Telugu Script | English Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Nenu bagunnanu | నేను బాగున్నాను | I am fine |
| Ela unnavu | ఎలా ఉన్నావు | How are you? |
| Intiki vastunnanu | ఇంటికి వస్తున్నాను | I am coming home |
| Amma, em chestunnavu? | అమ్మ, ఏం చేస్తున్నావు? | Mom, what are you doing? |

The primary goal is to correct Telugu words written using English letters, rather than translate English into Telugu or correct text written directly in Telugu script.

## Who Is Lekha For?

Lekha is designed for people who:

- Speak Telugu but struggle to read or write it in Telugu script.
- Prefer typing Telugu words using the English alphabet.
- Frequently message family and friends in Telugu.
- Find that English autocorrect changes their Telugu words or fails to recognize their intended spelling.

## The 5 Ws and 1 H

- **Who:** Telugu speakers who are more comfortable typing in English letters than writing in Telugu script.
- **What:** An AI-powered autocorrect system for Telugu words written using the English alphabet.
- **When:** Whenever users need to communicate in Telugu through text.
- **Where:** WhatsApp is the initial target platform.
- **Why:** To reduce communication barriers and make it easier for Telugu-speaking families to stay connected.
- **How:** By researching and experimenting with AI, machine learning, and natural language processing techniques for Telugu transliteration and personalized text correction.

## Project Goals

- Research existing transliteration and autocorrect tools for Telugu written in English letters.
- Explore AI/ML techniques for identifying and correcting misspelled Telugu words written using the English alphabet.
- Investigate how to learn individual users' spelling preferences and writing patterns.
- Reduce unwanted English autocorrections when users type Telugu words.
- Develop an accessible solution for everyday Telugu messaging.
- Explore practical integration options for a WhatsApp-based workflow.
- Evaluate correction accuracy, personalization, and usability.

## Development Roadmap

1. **Research:** Study existing transliteration tools, autocorrect systems, and challenges associated with Telugu written in English letters.
2. **Data and Experimentation:** Identify suitable datasets and investigate common Telugu transliterations and spelling variations.
3. **Prototype:** Develop an initial autocorrect system for transliterated Telugu text.
4. **Personalization:** Explore ways to adapt corrections to users' preferred spellings and writing patterns.
5. **Evaluation:** Test correction accuracy and gather feedback from Telugu speakers.
6. **Integration:** Investigate practical ways to use Lekha within a WhatsApp messaging workflow.

*This roadmap is preliminary and may evolve as the project progresses.*
