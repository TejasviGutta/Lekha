# Lekha

Ai Autocorrect for foreign languages with a focus on telugu
We will be using Ai and M/L

Who: Anyone who does not know how to read/write in their native language but can speak it. 
What: Ai powered autocorrect that learns from the user’s speech patterns
When: Based on user’s communication needs
Where: whatsapp
Why: ease communication gap between young adults in the US from their family back home. 
How: Research in progress
Problem: Autocorrect in english is a necessity for daily communication, however when speaking/messaging in a native language that is not english autocorrect gets in the way.
